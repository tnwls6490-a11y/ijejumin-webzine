About this page

  • The neighborhoods are grouped for browsing in this content, so they may differ from administrative boundaries.
  • Opening hours, admission fees and whether places are open to visitors were not checked for this page. Please check each place’s official information before you go. Yongmeori Coast in particular may be closed depending on the tides and weather.
